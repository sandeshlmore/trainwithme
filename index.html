THE WEEKLY SYSTEM

YOUR PROGRAM.

Push / Pull / Legs, twice through. Pick a day or use keys 1–7.

01 WARM UP +

Prepare to move well before you load up.

  1. 5–10 min light cardio
  2. Dynamic movement for the joints you will use
  3. 2–4 progressive warm-up sets on your first compound lift
Warm-up sets prepare you. They are not working sets.
02

HOW TO PROGRESS

Hit the top of the rep range with clean form on every set? Add a small amount of weight next session. If form breaks down, own the weight first.

03

RECOVERY MATTERS

  • Sleep 7–9 hours
  • Hydrate and eat for your goal
  • Respect rest days
  • Never train through sharp pain
